# SAP Activation Log: Portugal Digital Signature Web Services

## 1. ICF Service Activation (SICF)
To enable the Web Service communication layer for digital signing:
- **Transaction:** `SICF`
- **Path:** `default_host` > `sap` > `bc` > `webdynpro` > `sap` > `appl_soap_management`
- **Action:** Right-click `appl_soap_management` -> Activate Service (including hierarchy).
